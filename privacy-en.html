Privacy Policy

Version: November 12, 2026 · Effective: November 12, 2026 · 中文版

In short

  • Aiji Poetry (爱记古诗词) requires no account, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or push SDKs.
  • Family member nicknames, favorites, recitation progress, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to the developer. Only when you use Feedback are your message and screenshots sent to the developer.
  • The app goes online only when you choose to download an extra poetry pack (from a CDN) or use Family Circle (which syncs a check-in summary to your own WebDAV cloud drive).
  • Notification permission is requested only when you turn on the daily reminder.
  • On iOS, microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested only the first time you use "Hold to talk" in the Flying-Flower and Poem Chain games. Speech is converted to text on the device only and is never stored or uploaded.

Aiji Poetry ("the App", "we") takes your privacy seriously and processes information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable laws. This English version is provided for convenience; if it differs from the Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

1. What we process and why

1.1 Data stored on your device (not accessible to us)

  • Nicknames, avatar emoji and colors of local family members (we recommend nicknames, not real names);
  • Favorites, recitation progress, review schedule and daily check-ins;
  • Settings such as font size, theme, reading voice and reminder time;
  • Family Circle WebDAV address, username and password (only if you join a Family Circle).

This data is never uploaded to any server of ours. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

1.2 Downloading extra poetry packs

When you download a pack under "Me › Poetry Library", the App requests files from a content delivery network (CDN). The CDN provider keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, time and requested file) for delivery and security. We do not link these logs to you. No such request is made unless you download a pack.

1.3 Family Circle (optional)

  • What is synced: each local member's nickname, avatar, color, number of poems recited and mastered, check-in dates of the last 90 days, daily counts of the last 30 days, titles and authors of the 12 most recent poems, plus the circle name and random device IDs.
  • What is not synced: favorites, detailed progress and settings stay on the device.
  • Where it is stored: in your own WebDAV account (e.g. Jianguoyun), handled under that provider's policy. We cannot access it.
  • Invite codes contain the WebDAV credentials; share them only with family.
  • Leaving the circle deletes this device's file from the drive.

1.4 Daily reminder

When you enable it, the App asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on the device. No push server is involved.

1.5 Read aloud, voice input, sharing and copying

Read-aloud plays recitation audio bundled in the App or uses the system speech engine; the App does not record audio while reading aloud.

On iOS, the Flying-Flower and Poem Chain games offer "Hold to talk": while you hold the button, the App records audio and the built-in iOS speech recognizer converts it to text on the device. On-device recognition is required, so audio is never sent to Apple or any other server, and the App does not keep the recording. If the device does not support on-device Chinese recognition, the button does not record.

Sharing uses the system share sheet. A share image can be saved to Photos; this asks only for "add photos" access and never reads your photo library. Copying only writes the text you choose; the App never reads the clipboard.

1.6 Feedback (when you choose to use it)

When you submit feedback in "Me › Feedback", we receive what you write, any screenshots you attach, a random feedback ID generated by the App (used to route the developer's reply back to this device; it is not linked to your identity), and, if you leave the box checked, your app and system version. This is used only to reply to you and improve the App.

Feedback is stored with Supabase, the cloud service the developer uses (servers located outside mainland China). To route replies, the App creates an anonymous Supabase identity that contains no personal information. Only the developer can see all feedback; other users cannot see yours. Screenshots are stored under hard-to-guess random links and are never listed publicly. Feedback is kept for no more than 12 months after it is resolved, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Please do not include phone numbers, ID numbers or other sensitive information. The App does not contact this service unless you use Feedback.

1.7 AI-generated content

Some translations, notes and appreciations were generated with AI assistance before release, are bundled in the App, and are labeled as such. No information about you is sent to any AI service.

3. Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, push, login or payment SDKs are included. Open-source components (Capacitor and its plugins, SQLite, the system speech API) run locally and send nothing to third parties. Feedback is stored using Supabase's database and storage services (Supabase acts only as a storage provider). We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

On-device data is protected by the system app sandbox. Network traffic uses HTTPS. Apart from feedback you choose to submit (stored on Supabase servers outside mainland China, see 1.6), we do not store or transfer your information outside mainland China. You can email us instead of using Feedback.

5. Your rights

  • View and edit member nicknames in "Me › Members"; delete members, favorites, or leave the Family Circle at any time. To delete feedback you submitted, ask us in the feedback thread or by email.
  • Withdraw consent in "Me › About › Withdraw privacy consent".
  • Turn off notification, microphone, speech recognition or photo permissions in system settings at any time.
  • There is no account, so no account deletion is needed.

6. Children

The App is designed for families, with children using it under parental guidance. We do not collect children's personal information. Use nicknames rather than real names, and a parent should create and manage the Family Circle.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the App will ask you to review and accept it again.
