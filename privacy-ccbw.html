Privacy Policy

Version: November 15, 2026 · Effective: November 15, 2026 · 中文版

In short

  • Word by Word (词词不忘) requires no account, shows no ads, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or push SDKs. The dictionary, illustrations and audio are bundled, so studying works offline.
  • Your study records (which words you learned, every answer, review schedule), word states and settings are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to the developer automatically.
  • The app goes online only when you choose to turn on cloud backup or Study Circle (both talk only to your own WebDAV drive) or use Feedback (your message and screenshots are sent to the developer).
  • Notification permission is requested only when you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested only the first time you use the "Speak" exercise. Speech is recognized on the device only and is never uploaded.

Word by Word ("the App", "we") takes your privacy seriously and processes information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable laws. This English version is provided for convenience; if it differs from the Chinese version, the Chinese version prevails.

1. What we process and why

1.1 Data stored on your device (not accessible to us)

  • Study records: each word's memory state, the time and result of every answer, the review schedule and daily statistics;
  • Settings such as word list, daily new words, gestures, pronunciation, reminder time and theme;
  • If you use Study Circle: your nickname, avatar emoji and color (use a nickname, not your real name) and the WebDAV address, username and app password of the drive; if you turn on cloud backup: the same kind of drive credentials;
  • A random device ID generated by the App (to tell your phones apart; it is not linked to your identity and no system device identifier is read).

This data is not uploaded to any server of ours automatically, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

1.2 Automatic cloud backup (optional)

When on, the App packs your study records and settings into a backup file and uploads it to the WebDAV drive you enter (e.g. Jianguoyun), together with a safety copy and a small note recording which device uploaded how many words and when. The backup has the same content as "Export backup": the words you studied and your answer history, but not the drive password. The files live in your own drive account under that provider's policy, and we cannot access them. If you do not turn it on, the App makes no backup-related network requests. You can turn it off at any time and delete the files from your drive.

1.3 Study Circle (optional)

Study Circle lets classmates or family see each other's check-ins and rankings. It also uses your own WebDAV drive; nothing passes through our servers.

  • What is synced: your nickname, avatar, color, current word list name, number of words learned, stable-memory and long-term-memory counts, which of the last 90 days you studied, daily answer and new-word counts for the last 30 days, plus the circle name and random device IDs.
  • What is not synced: which specific words you studied, per-answer details and settings stay on the device.
  • Invite codes contain the drive's WebDAV address, username and app password; anyone who has one can read and write the circle's files. Share them only with people you trust, and we recommend registering a dedicated drive account for the circle, not one that holds your backups or private files.
  • Where it is stored: in the drive account, under that provider's policy. Circle members can see the summary above.
  • Leaving the circle deletes this device's file from the drive.

If you do not join a circle, the App makes no circle-related network requests.

1.4 Daily reminder

When you enable it, the App asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications for the coming days (for example "N words are due today", sometimes naming one new word). No push server is involved. You can turn it off in the App or in system settings at any time.

1.5 Pronunciation and the "Speak" exercise

Pronunciation plays audio bundled in the App or uses the system speech engine; the App does not record audio while playing pronunciation. On some devices the speech engine is supplied by a third-party vendor and is governed by that vendor's policy.

The "Speak" exercise records audio after you start it, and the system's built-in speech recognizer checks your pronunciation on the device. On-device recognition is required, so audio is never sent to any server. The recording is kept only temporarily in the app cache so you can compare, then replaced or deleted; it is not included in backups and is never uploaded. If the device does not support on-device recognition, the exercise does not appear and nothing is recorded.

1.6 Feedback (when you choose to use it)

When you submit feedback in "Me › Feedback", we receive what you write, any screenshots you attach, a random feedback ID generated by the App (used to route the developer's reply back to this device; it is not linked to your identity), and, if you leave the box checked, your app and system version. This is used only to reply to you and improve the App.

Feedback is stored with Supabase, the cloud service the developer uses (servers located outside mainland China). To route replies, the App creates an anonymous Supabase identity that contains no personal information. Only the developer can see all feedback; other users cannot see yours. Screenshots are stored under hard-to-guess random links and are never listed publicly. Feedback is kept for no more than 12 months after it is resolved, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Please do not include phone numbers, ID numbers or other sensitive information. The App does not contact this service unless you use Feedback.

1.7 Export, sharing and clipboard

"Export backup" creates a file and opens the system share sheet; where it goes is your choice. "Restore" reads only the backup file you pick. The App never reads the clipboard and never reads your photo library (only the single image you choose to attach in Feedback is read).

1.8 AI-generated content

Some definitions, example sentences, illustrations and audio were generated with AI before release and are bundled in the App. No information about you is sent to any AI service.

3. Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, push, login or payment SDKs are included. Open-source components (Capacitor and its plugins, SQLite, the system speech APIs) run locally and send nothing to third parties. Feedback is stored using Supabase's database and storage services (Supabase acts only as a storage provider). Cloud backup and Study Circle data go only to the drive provider you enter. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

  • On-device data is protected by the system app sandbox and kept until you uninstall the App. Drive credentials are kept in the app's local storage and are not included in backups.
  • Data in your drive is stored by the drive provider you choose. Network traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Apart from feedback you choose to submit (stored on Supabase servers outside mainland China, see 1.6), we do not store or transfer your information outside mainland China. You can email us instead of using Feedback.

5. Your rights

  • View and edit your nickname and avatar in "Me › Study Circle"; browse your records in "Words" and "Report".
  • Clear your study records, leave a circle, or turn off backup and delete the files from your drive at any time. Uninstalling the App deletes all on-device data. To delete feedback you submitted, ask us in the feedback thread or by email.
  • Withdraw consent in "Me › About › Withdraw privacy consent". After withdrawing you must accept the policy again to keep using the App. Withdrawal does not affect processing done earlier on the basis of consent and does not delete your study records.
  • Turn off notification, microphone or speech recognition permissions in system settings at any time.
  • There is no account, so no account deletion is needed.

We reply within 15 working days.

6. Children

The App is suitable for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and do not ask for names or phone numbers; we recommend a nickname rather than a child's real name in Study Circle. Minors should read this policy with a parent or guardian, and a parent or adult should set up and manage Study Circle and cloud backup; do not let children forward invite codes freely.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the App will ask you to review and accept it again.
